Teaching Capability

Teaching approach

Teaching is organised around alignment between learning outcomes, worked demonstrations, assessment and feedback. Abstract control, modelling and AI concepts are made observable through simulation, laboratory systems and project-based engineering tasks. Students are encouraged to connect mathematical reasoning with experimental evidence, implementation choices and defensible engineering decisions.

Research Profile

Research centres on AI-enabled engineering systems that combine sensing, modelling and decision support. The programme links fundamental methods with deployable systems and industry needs across critical infrastructure, non-destructive evaluation, robotics and asset management.

AI and engineering data

Predictive modelling, maintenance-data analysis, explainable risk assessment and decision-support systems.

Computer vision and signal processing

Multispectral imaging, spectral unmixing, object tracking, defect detection and quantitative image analysis.

Intelligent sensing and robotics

Multisensor platforms, embedded acquisition, UAV inspection, structural monitoring and robotic navigation.

Physics and reliability

Finite element modelling, physics-informed learning, stochastic analysis and experimentally informed reliability assessment.
